Heart Attack Prediction using Retinal Images

Abstract

Abstract

The system for heart attack risk prediction using retinal eye images utilizes machine learning (ML) and artificial intelligence (AI) techniques to enhance early detection of cardiovascular risks. By analyzing retinal blood vessels, the system identifies subtle changes caused by hypertension and heart disease that are invisible to the human eye. Advanced AI models perform vessel segmentation to isolate and analyze blood vessel structures, ensuring precise and efficient detection of preclinical markers. This innovative approach supports timely interventions, reduces the risk of severe cardiac events, and strengthens preventive healthcare measures. By leveraging AI and ML, the system ensures accurate, reliable, and scalable heart disease risk prediction, fostering greater confidence in modern diagnostic methods.
